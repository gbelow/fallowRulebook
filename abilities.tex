\section{Acquiring abilities}

Abilities are acquired through XP during downtime. To calculate XP cost of an ability, use the rules from character creation.

Abilities can only be acquired once. When a technique has multiple levels, it receives an I, II, III next to its name, indicating each level. Each level has a different training level, which means the talent requirement changes. 

An ability can have its bonuses written as 1/2/3 or similar. That means that at each level, they get upgraded to the corresponding stage. An effect written as I. II. III. has one segment per level. A single value applies to every level and is paid at each.

\textbf{Usage:} \textit{passive} is always on. A cost (\textit{2 AP}, \textit{1 STA}, \textit{2 AP + 1 STA}, \textit{1 ET}, \textit{2 IL}) is an action; \textit{attack} costs whatever the attack costs. \textit{reaction} is used outside the character's turn, with its cost after the comma. \textit{sustained} is paid per turn while active. \textit{full turn} and \textit{ritual} take the whole turn or a ritual.

\textbf{Cost:} the price, followed by the talent and training level when the price is XP. \textit{6 XP, DEX 3} costs 6 XP trained at level 3 with DEX as talent. Karma prices have no talent. \textit{0 XP} means the ability is gained as soon as its requirements are met, unless its text says otherwise. A negative price is received, not paid, when the ability is taken. Values split by / are per level.

\textbf{Requirements:} it is not possible to acquire an ability unless the requirements are met. All listed items are needed; \textit{or} gives alternatives within an item and \textit{not} forbids one. Items are abilities (with their level, \textit{Riding I}), skills or knowledges at a level (\textit{Survival 2}), attribute thresholds (\textit{STR > 15}), or a condition (\textit{racial}, \textit{ritual}).

\section{\magic Mutations}

Mutations are unusual physical transformations that are either innate or acquired through magical means in games with magic. When acquired, they involve activating dormant genes or implanting enhancements, and gardening growth to achieve a certain form. They are triggered in the body by traumatic events, scientific intervention, divine influence and sheer will to change.

Usually, a mutation is learned by spending Karma, but can also be learned through Biomancy. 

\textbf{Learning through biomancy:} A Health test that allows transforming the body.
